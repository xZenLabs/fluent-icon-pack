Monochrome SVG bodies and native geometry are retained from Iconify's
collection at revision c455eb6232ac0fa657550afb512af55fb8237ed6:
https://github.com/iconify/icon-sets/blob/c455eb6232ac0fa657550afb512af55fb8237ed6/json/fluent.json

Changes: wrapped the Iconify SVG bodies in standalone SVG documents, renamed
files for supported lookups, and rotated or mirrored selected arrows and markers.
The regular outline style is used, with a filled star for the full-star
state. SVG currentColor is explicitly black; alpha variants reduce opacity.
Native view boxes and path geometry are preserved. Similar designs are reused
for related actions. Unavailable controls retain the bundled interface artwork.
Fluent Mono/icon-sources.json records every source name, rotation, checksum, and fallback.
The Python builder generates additional aliases without changing the SVGs.
